# PhotonHash v0.3 boundary phase-one decision

Status: **frozen negative research decision; no consensus status**
Date: 2026-07-26
Branch: `research/v0.3`

## Decision

**No evaluated boundary class is an evidence-qualified 3x survivor. R1 does
not advance beyond conditional architecture status, and the feed-forward
family is not superior. Both are rejected for v0.3 design work on the present
evidence.**

This is not a proof that a qualifying physical boundary is impossible. It is
the charter result for the boundary classes and evidence available in this
phase:

- unassisted low-resolution regeneration is cheap but its exact trajectory
  yield collapses;
- a full ADC/digital/remodulation loop preserves modeled exactness but loses
  the economic gate;
- selective digital assistance is the only tested R1 class with favorable
  numerical behavior, but its conservative complete-system result is 1.86x,
  not 3x, and no representative boundary demonstrates its assumed error and
  energy terms;
- three-lane redundancy improves yield but multiplies the dominant matrix
  energy and reaches only 0.60x conservatively;
- measured all-optical nonlinearities are promising neural-network devices,
  but they do not implement an exact wrap map and their plausible analog
  error produces effectively zero complete-trajectory yield;
- feed-forward stages remove repeated full conversion only by retaining the
  same unresolved exact nonlinear boundary in the optical path, while adding
  an ungrounded physical matrix/stage bank.

The strongest remaining numerical hypothesis is a two-round, large-dimension
R1 recurrence with selective exact assistance. It is a **target envelope**, not
a surviving candidate.

## Governing metric

This phase uses joules per valid byte-exact attempt:

~~~text
J_valid = J_nominal_complete_attempt / P_exact

J_nominal_complete_attempt =
    J_matrix
  + J_boundary
  + J_control
  + J_hashing
  + J_assist
  + J_recheck
~~~

`P_exact` is the probability that the complete linked trajectory matches the
integer reference state. An incorrect but successfully completed optical pass
is a failed attempt and its energy is charged through the denominator.

For an equal-power system with enough independent lanes, the energy advantage
is also the ideal equal-power valid-throughput ratio. The simulator separately
reports a single-lane latency ratio; current photonic latency inputs are not
prototype-grounded and therefore cannot establish the throughput gate.

## Exactness requirement

The provisional R1 boundary has 256 exact accumulator residues inside every
output bucket. Some valid sums lie exactly at a bucket boundary, so a physical
implementation needs a deterministic threshold bias or a trusted ambiguity
path. Treating margins as a continuous uniform distribution would hide this
problem; the simulator enumerates all 256 residues.

The equal-work R1 frontier makes the following number of linked nonlinear
decisions. If errors were independent, the maximum per-decision error for a
95% exact-attempt yield would be:

| Dimension | Rounds | Decisions/attempt | Maximum error for 95% yield |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 8192 | 2 | 16,384 | 3.13e-6 |
| 4096 | 8 | 32,768 | 1.57e-6 |
| 2048 | 32 | 65,536 | 7.83e-7 |
| 1024 | 128 | 131,072 | 3.91e-7 |

Correlated offset, drift, temperature, and calibration failures are worse than
this independent-error calculation because lane replication and averaging do
not remove a shared error.

## Simulator

`tools/ph_v03_boundary_sim.py` performs deterministic-seed Monte Carlo over:

- dimension, round count, boundary count, and exact residue precision;
- independent symbol noise;
- per-boundary common-mode noise;
- drift that grows with boundary age;
- temperature and component offset;
- physical threshold bias and zero-margin ties;
- selective guard bands and assistance success probability;
- residual error floors and per-boundary calibration failures;
- one- or three-lane state representations;
- matrix, boundary, assistance, fixed, and latency uncertainty ranges.

For every residue and sampled boundary condition, the tool integrates the
probability of landing in the correct unassisted interval, an assisted guard
interval, or an undetected wrong interval. It then composes logical symbol
success over `n` outputs and all boundaries in log space. The energy result is
divided by that complete exact yield.

The main result uses 5,000 uncertainty/trajectory samples per profile. The
focused sensitivity grid uses 2,000 samples per profile. Unit tests cover
deterministic ties, exponential yield loss, assistance, failed-assistance
charging, redundancy validation, and seeded reproducibility.

## Main results

Fifth percentile is the charter's conservative tail. `P50` is median exact
trajectory yield; `A05` and `A50` are complete-system energy advantages after
yield. Values below machine range are reported as zero.

| Boundary/profile | P_exact p05 | P_exact median | J_valid median (uJ) | A05 | A50 | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| R1 low-res, unassisted, 8192 x 2 | 1.68e-19 | 2.16e-10 | 3.76e11 | 5.47e-19x | 6.76e-10x | reject: yield |
| R1 selective assist, 8192 x 2 | 0.99825 | 0.999978 | 80.61 | 1.86x | 3.11x | reject: Gate 2/evidence |
| R1 selective assist, 4096 x 8 | 0.29541 | 0.999945 | 85.85 | 0.93x | 3.05x | reject: tail drift |
| R1 selective assist, 2048 x 32 | 1.36e-121 | 3.66e-10 | 2.73e11 | 3.76e-121x | 1.07e-9x | reject: yield |
| R1 selective assist, 1024 x 128 | underflow | underflow | infinity | 0x | 0x | reject: yield |
| R1 full digital roundtrip, 8192 x 2 | 0.999872 | 0.999944 | 144.01 | 1.10x | 1.74x | reject: energy |
| R1 three-lane majority, 8192 x 2 | 0.669 | 0.902 | 222.34 | 0.60x | 1.13x | reject: energy/yield |
| F1 all-optical, 8192 x 2 | 3.23e-247 | 1.11e-132 | 1.02e134 | 1.18e-246x | 2.52e-132x | reject: exactness |
| F1 all-optical, 4096 x 8 | underflow | underflow | infinity | 0x | 0x | reject: exactness |
| R1 projected target envelope | 0.999984 | 0.999992 | 31.63 | 7.17x | 7.93x | numeric only; not evidence-qualified |

The selective 8192 x 2 profile sent a median 2.26% of lane decisions to
assistance. Its median nominal energy was 80.46 uJ and median yield-adjusted
energy was 80.61 uJ. Yield is not the limiting median term in that profile;
the broad R1-scale matrix, fixed-support, boundary, and control uncertainty
makes the conservative advantage only 1.86x.

The full digital roundtrip used a 98%-wide assistance band to represent every
symbol paying a trusted conversion path. It maintained median exact yield of
0.999944 but fell to 1.10x conservatively and 1.74x at the median. This
quantifies why consensus exactness alone is not enough.

## Sensitivity findings

The fixed sensitivity point uses a 247 uJ electronic attempt, 33.554 uJ
matrix energy (250 fJ/MAC), 18 uJ fixed support, and a 10 pJ boundary decision.
It is a mechanism probe, not evidence of an R1 photonic system.

| Change | Median P_exact | Advantage |
|---|---:|---:|
| independent sigma 0.0001 bucket | 1.0 | 4.776x |
| independent sigma 0.0005 bucket | 0.9940 | 4.747x |
| independent sigma 0.001 bucket | 0.0387 | 0.185x |
| independent sigma 0.0025 bucket | 2.37e-13 | 1.13e-12x |
| 0.0025-bucket guard at sigma 0.001 | 0.99946 | 4.765x |
| 0.005-bucket guard at sigma 0.001 | 1.0 | 4.762x |
| assist success 0.999999 | 0.99967 | 4.744x |
| assist success 0.9999 | 0.96736 | 4.591x |
| 8 rounds with 0.001/boundary drift | median 1.0; catastrophic p05 | 5.25e-14x p05 |
| 32 rounds with 0.001/boundary drift | 1.49e-183 | 6.86e-183x |

This supplies an immediate kill rule: without a local correction mechanism,
an effective residual error around 1e-3 per bucket is already fatal even at
two rounds. Guarded selective correction changes that scaling, but correction
must itself be exact to much better than one failure in a million and must fit
inside the complete energy budget.

## Energy gates

At the 247 uJ electronic median, complete photonic budgets are:

| Gate | Maximum complete photonic energy |
|---|---:|
| 3x survival | 82.33 uJ |
| 5x serious candidate | 49.40 uJ |
| 10x strong result | 24.70 uJ |

At 250 fJ/MAC, the 134,217,728-MAC matrix alone costs 33.554 uJ. Adding the
18 uJ fixed sensitivity budget already reaches 51.554 uJ before boundary and
assistance, so that case cannot reach 5x even with perfect yield. It does show
a narrow numerical route above 3x.

The projected target envelope constrains the matrix to 18-25 uJ, fixed work to
9-12 uJ, boundary energy to 0.01-0.13 uJ, and selective assistance to roughly
0.14-0.62 uJ. It reaches 7.17x conservatively. Every decisive term is a target
or transferred projection, so the result has no Gate 2 admission weight.

## Candidate dispositions

### Low-bit hard threshold or sign regeneration

For the existing R1 wrap operation, an unassisted comparator is eliminated by
exact yield. A sign or ternary state would change the construction and is not
an R1 boundary; it remains outside this phase and cannot be used to claim that
R1 survived.

### Regenerative comparators and latches

The comparator element can be extremely cheap. The missing object is the
complete detector-reference-latch-route-drive-modulator path with a
deterministic tie rule, correlated-error characterization, and linked symbolic
BER. Component energy alone does not pass the gate.

### Low-resolution conversion with selective assistance

This is the only class with a plausible numerical path. At two rounds it can
avoid exponential loss with a 0.5-2% bucket guard, but the conservative
complete-system result is below 3x. Its projected 7.17x envelope defines a
prototype target, not a candidate result.

### Redundant or error-correcting state

Three full matrix lanes are economically rejected. More sophisticated local
codes could avoid tripling every MAC, but no exact code, decoder path, or
physical energy model was supplied. Such a code would be a new boundary
invention and must be evaluated from scratch.

### Physically grounded optical nonlinearities

Existing devices show excellent threshold energy and speed, but none
demonstrates the exact R1 map or the required symbolic error rate. Applying
their analog-error range without trusted regeneration yields effectively zero
valid attempts. This class is rejected for R1.

### Feed-forward optical stages

Feed-forward is not superior under this phase. It avoids repeated full
optical-electrical-optical crossings only if an exact optical nonlinearity
exists; the evidence does not provide one. The GPU already executes distinct
resident matrices without penalty, while the optical design must instantiate
or program a large stage bank. Both tested feed-forward profiles fail exact
yield by overwhelming margins.

## What evidence would change the result

R1 should not resume design work unless a representative boundary pipeline
demonstrates all of the following at the same declared operating point:

1. the exact 4-bit wrap transition, including deterministic zero-margin ties;
2. residual undetected error below roughly 3e-6 per logical decision for the
   8192 x 2 corner, with a substantially lower target for high-yield operation;
3. measured independent and correlated error across voltage, temperature,
   drift, calibration age, and device variation;
4. complete detector-through-remodulator energy, including guard decisions,
   assistance, references, routing, clocks, control, failed trajectories, and
   rechecks;
5. complete projected energy below 82.33 uJ for 3x and below 49.40 uJ for 5x
   at the 247 uJ comparison point, with conservative uncertainty;
6. at least 2x valid throughput at equal board power; and
7. a scaling argument from the representative pipeline to 8192 state symbols
   and 134,217,728 MACs without silently dropping fanout, loss, area, storage,
   or calibration.

Until such evidence exists, the appropriate state is:

~~~text
R1: archived conditional architecture; does not advance
F1 feed-forward: rejected as a superior alternative
PhotonHash v0.3 design/specification: not started
Laser consensus: unchanged
v0.2: frozen
~~~
