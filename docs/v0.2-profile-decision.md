# v0.2 profile decision

**Decision:** do not activate or freeze PhotonHash v0.2 in Laser consensus.

The n=1024 construction remains useful as an exact experimental reference,
but its primary hardware-separation requirement R3 is falsified by the
balanced RTX 5090 baseline:

- exact matrix work is 11.06-12.21% of sequential device time;
- the best measured complete pipeline is 33.43 million attempts/s;
- overlap improves throughput without creating matrix dominance;
- a free matrix stage would cap speedup near 1.14x at the saturated batch;
- simple low-rank and column-skipping experiments found no exact shortcut,
  so the failure is economic balance rather than a demonstrated consensus
  break.

This decision is deliberately not a speculative v0.3 parameter bump. The
measured cost model requires roughly 66 non-redundant matrix-equivalent units
to reach the old 90% target. Repeated identical work, dozens of separately
loaded matrices, or a roughly 65x dimension increase each defeats a core
design premise.

## Reopening gates

A successor may be proposed only with all of the following:

1. a byte-exact independently written implementation and expanded vectors;
2. an optimized electronic baseline including batch overlap and all digital
   stages, measured before parameters are frozen;
3. a complete photonic measurement including modulation, detection, ADC,
   guard-band classification, digital assist, hashing, and candidate recheck;
4. a construction-level reason the optical share survives electronic
   batching, not merely a scalar operation count;
5. matrix-corpus and approximation tests at the proposed parameters;
6. verifier cost, caching, DoS, and work-discontinuity analysis;
7. explicit activation criteria and a rollback-safe testnet phase.

Until those gates pass, repository language must describe PhotonHash as an
experimental and currently falsified profile, not as demonstrated
photonic-advantaged proof of work.
