# Qualification decision memo: C20 follow-up

**30 October 2026 UTC. Recommendation: REVISE. Candidate freeze withheld.**
This is evidence for the team's qualification decision. It authorizes no Laser
activation, release, block interval or consensus change. Experiment domain tags
and the mathematical construction remain unchanged.

**Established within the stated tests.** The existing measured canonical set
has 560 cases agreeing in C, CUDA, outside Python and Rust, with full folds;
the established measured random replay has 1,048,576 C/GPU cases and no skips.
The new GPU passes the canonical packed/fused checks and required binding
boundaries. New exact OpenSSL bank generation matches portable C, including
full coefficients, seeds and audit checksums. A serialized admission laboratory
rejects its hostile function-call batches before bank work and passes sanitizer
checks. The telemetry repair rejects the historical excessive gap and cannot
refresh a failed heartbeat back to healthy. Exact modular accumulation has a
formal residue proof and passing mapped-netlist equivalence checks.

**New measured economics, with limits.** The separate server GPU completes the
fixed unpaced cold/warm confirmation and supplies continuously sampled board
energy, MAD and inferred sampling intervals. It does not replace an RTX 5090
retry, explain that board's old discrepancy, or calibrate NVML. Isolated stage
measurements include the epilogue and are exploratory; pure GEMM arithmetic
time/energy remains unisolated. Optimized bank generation and measured worker
scaling reduce the inferred hash-only sync and turnover costs on the recorded
cloud CPUs. A shared modern server allocation is not physical modest/low-power
qualification. Cell-library mapping gives an inferred arithmetic-area proxy,
not ASIC energy, manufacturable timing closure or whole-miner cost.

**Failures and evidence boundaries.** Earlier thermal/telemetry aborts, the
invalid fused-LF2 series and the Windows display-loss incident remain on the
record. They are not repaired by a successful separate cohort. The current
pre-analysis NumPy import failure is retained; it occurred before data processing.
The first packaging attempt also failed on a sampler-copy path; its log and
receipt remain, followed by the corrected packaging attempt. Measurements were
not repeated. Source/build/pilot receipts and raw sensor records are preserved.
CUDA kernel sources remain outside this pre-freeze publication, limiting public
GPU reproduction until the authorized source-publication step.

**Security interpretation.** No tested method demonstrates a work-avoiding
energy-per-exact-fold advantage. Approximation can preserve a fold; the relevant
criterion is measured energy divided by exact-fold probability. Existing noisy
and pruning studies are finite, with explicitly inferred energy assumptions.
They do not rule out physical analog hardware, correlated errors, cheaper
preprocessing, vector/parent grinding or sparse-specialized execution. Narrow
exact arithmetic is a hardware-economics result, not an approximation failure.

**Required next decision evidence.** Obtain outside cryptographic/grinding and
hardware review using the published review scope; resolve independent power
metrology and cooled 5090 confirmation; measure a physical modest/low-power
verifier; and qualify actual network admission, scheduling/fairness and hostile
traffic. Investigate Windows separately without local research authorization.
Mapped ASIC power and full-bank photonic loading/noise/complete-system energy
remain open. The standalone laboratory is preparation for production integration,
not evidence of live Laser admission safety.

Header serialization/length, difficulty adjustment/work score, genesis difficulty,
timestamp rules, block time and activation remain **OPEN team integration items**.
If the remaining qualification supports freezing, the later package must set
candidate tags, regenerate vectors and rate boundaries, publish the normative
specification and reference paths, and issue the immutable freeze manifest.
This work publishes transport manifests only.
